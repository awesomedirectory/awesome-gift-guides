# Awesome Gift Guides [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of gift guides, gift finders, wishlist and registry tools, and gifting services — for when you have a name, a budget, and no idea.

Most gift shopping starts from the recipient, not the product: who it is for (for example, [gifts for a wife](https://ribbonworthy.com/for/wife)), roughly what to spend, and whether they already said what they want. This list covers the editorial guides people actually read, tools that narrow the choice or collect wishes in one place, ways for a group to give together, and shops that do the curating for you.

## Editorial Gift Guides
*Lists written by shopping and review desks, refreshed every holiday season.*

* [CNET Gift Guide](https://www.cnet.com/gifts/) - Gift roundups from CNET's editors, weighted toward tech and gadgets, organized by recipient and price.
* [Good Housekeeping](https://www.goodhousekeeping.com/holidays/gift-ideas/) - Gift idea lists by recipient, occasion, and interest from the magazine's editors.
* [Real Simple](https://www.realsimple.com/holidays-entertaining/gifts) - Practical gift lists for hosts, family, coworkers, and specific occasions.
* [Reviewed](https://www.reviewed.com/gift-guides) - USA Today network's product review site; gift guides drawn from its category coverage.
* [The Strategist](https://nymag.com/strategist/gifts/) - New York magazine's shopping site; gift lists often built from recommendations by named people.
* [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/gift-guides) - Tech-focused gift guides from The Verge's reviews team, by recipient and budget.
* [Wired](https://www.wired.com/tag/gift-guides/) - Gear desk gift guides covering gadgets, outdoor equipment, and hobbies.
* [Wirecutter](https://www.nytimes.com/wirecutter/gifts/) - The New York Times' product recommendation site; gift guides drawn from its existing picks.

## Visual & Interactive Gift Guides
*Guides you browse by looking rather than scrolling a list.*

* [NPR Books We Love](https://apps.npr.org/best-books/) - Filterable grid of book covers picked by NPR staff and critics each year; useful for book gifts.
* [Pinterest Ideas](https://www.pinterest.com/ideas/) - Image boards and searchable idea pages; gift searches surface user-pinned collections.
* [RibbonWorthy](https://ribbonworthy.com) - Gift guide laid out as 3D shelves, one per recipient (wife, husband, mum, dad, kids, friends and more), where every pick is a product you can spin around before you click through. Filters by budget band; picks cite the editorial outlets that recommended them.

## Gift Finders & Recommendation Tools
*Answer a few questions or set a few filters, get a shortlist.*

* [Amazon Gift Finder](https://www.amazon.com/gp/gift-finder) - Amazon's gift browse pages, filtered by recipient, interest, and price.
* [Etsy Gift Mode](https://www.etsy.com/gift-mode) - Asks about the recipient and occasion, then suggests Etsy listings grouped by theme.
* [Uncommon Goods Gift Finder](https://www.uncommongoods.com/gifts/gift-finder) - Narrows the Uncommon Goods catalog by recipient, occasion, interest, and price.

## Wishlists & Registries
*Let people say what they want, and stop two people buying the same thing.*

* [Amazon Wish List](https://www.amazon.com/hz/wishlist/intro) - Shareable lists of Amazon items, with an option to add items from other sites.
* [Babylist](https://www.babylist.com) - Baby registry that accepts items from any store, plus cash funds.
* [DreamList](https://www.dreamlist.com) - Wishlist and registry maker for birthdays, holidays, weddings, and babies.
* [Giftful](https://giftful.com) - Wishlists with an iOS app and browser extension; friends and family can claim items to avoid duplicates.
* [Giftster](https://www.giftster.com) - Family wishlist app built around groups, with hidden claiming so gifts stay a surprise.
* [MyRegistry](https://www.myregistry.com) - Universal registry for weddings, babies, and other occasions, collecting items from any retailer.
* [Throne](https://throne.com) - Wishlists for creators and streamers that keep the owner's shipping address private from gifters.

## Group Gifting & Gift Exchanges
*Secret Santa draws, shared cards, and pooled gifts.*

* [DrawNames](https://www.drawnames.com) - Secret Santa generator with wishlists and a gift finder; no registration needed to draw.
* [Elfster](https://www.elfster.com) - Gift exchange organizer with name drawing, wishlists, and invitations by email or app.
* [GroupGreeting](https://www.groupgreeting.com) - Group e-cards that many people sign, often paired with a pooled gift card.
* [Kudoboard](https://www.kudoboard.com) - Shared online boards of messages, photos, and videos for birthdays, farewells, and milestones.
* [Secret Santa Organizer](https://www.secretsantaorganizer.com) - Free name draw with exclusions (for example, keeping couples apart) and emailed assignments, no account required.

## Gift Cards & Experiences
*For when the right gift is a choice, or something to do rather than own.*

* [Buyagift](https://www.buyagift.co.uk) - UK experience days, spa breaks, and dining vouchers.
* [GiftCards.com](https://www.giftcards.com) - Physical and digital gift cards for hundreds of brands, plus customizable Visa and Mastercard cards.
* [Prezzee](https://www.prezzee.com) - Digital gift cards delivered by email or text, including multi-brand cards the recipient can split.
* [Red Letter Days](https://www.redletterdays.co.uk) - UK experience gift vouchers, from driving days to afternoon teas.
* [Tinggly](https://www.tinggly.com) - Experience gift boxes where the recipient chooses an activity from a catalog spanning many countries.
* [Virgin Experience Gifts](https://www.virgingifts.com) - US experience gifts such as flights, driving, food tours, and classes; absorbed Cloud 9 Living.
* [Xperience Days](https://www.xperiencedays.com) - US experience gift certificates for flight lessons, race car driving, food tours, and similar activities.

## Curated Gift Shops
*Shops whose selection is the point.*

* [Bespoke Post](https://www.bespokepost.com) - Themed boxes and a shop of goods for cooking, drinking, grooming, and the outdoors.
* [Boxfox](https://www.boxfox.com) - Pre-made and build-your-own gift boxes, with a corporate gifting arm.
* [Cratejoy](https://www.cratejoy.com) - Marketplace of subscription boxes from independent sellers, giftable by the month.
* [Goldbelly](https://www.goldbelly.com) - Ships food from well-known restaurants and bakeries across the US.
* [Not On The High Street](https://www.notonthehighstreet.com) - UK marketplace of small independent sellers, strong on personalized gifts.
* [Sugarwish](https://www.sugarwish.com) - Sends a link to a box of treats; the recipient picks the contents.
* [Uncommon Goods](https://www.uncommongoods.com) - Catalog of unusual, often handmade or independent-designer gifts.
